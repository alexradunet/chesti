### 2. Late inherited-disabled result is ignored

### 3. Stale lookup retargeting, abort, error and cancellation

Separately simulate a lookup failure, assert visible error and unchanged selects, then real Escape and focus return. Restore fetch/state or dispose that owned tab. ### 4. General Ctrl+K navigation

### 5. Formatted-writing bookmark, exact placement, one undo and Escape
